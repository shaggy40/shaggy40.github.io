Credit riskGrayQuestPython · IFRS 9

An early warning system for a ₹80.76 Cr loan book

Collections was reactive — a parent missed an instalment, then someone called. I built a probability-of-default scorecard on 24,626 applications, staged the live book under Ind AS 109, and turned the scores into a ranked worklist sorted by expected loss. Logistic regression over a tree ensemble on purpose: a credit officer has to tell a parent why they were declined, and a coefficient does that where a black box can't.

The finding that mattered most wasn't statistical. 233 repeat defaulters were holding live loans and only 23% had been through a personal discussion before re-approval — a process gap fixable in a week, with no model required.

ValuationCAPM · WACC

Why three gas distributors cost different money

I estimated the cost of capital for three listed City Gas Distribution firms, expecting capital structure to explain the spread. It didn't — the difference traced almost entirely to equity beta, the market's read on each firm's regulatory and volume risk. At an 18% hurdle rate you reject projects a 14.4% peer would fund.

Hello — I'm Shagun

I did a B.Tech in computer science and then went looking for problems where the code was the easy part. Credit risk turned out to be exactly that: the modelling is well-trodden, and the hard question is always what a business should do with a number once it has one.

It's the fastest way I know to learn a product — and a business model.

I'm in the second year of my PGDM at Great Lakes, Gurgaon, working towards analytics and risk roles in financial services. What I enjoy sits at the join — a model that changes a workflow, a valuation that changes an investment decision, a price that changes a demand curve.
